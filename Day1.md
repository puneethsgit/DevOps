# Day 1

## What is DevOps?  
DevOps is a culture, methodology, and set of practices that aim to enhance the collaboration between development (Dev) and operations (Ops) teams. It focuses on automating processes and streamlining workflows to enable faster, more reliable delivery of applications and updates.  

### Example:  
Consider a popular application like PUBG. Without DevOps practices, delivering a new update or fixing a bug could take 10-15 days due to lengthy manual processes. However, with DevOps, these processes are automated, making it faster to deploy fixes and updates, thereby improving the application's overall delivery speed and reliability.
